Depending on the features you choose and the permissions Meta grants, the app may access information such as your public profile and account identifier, the Pages you manage, Page names and identifiers, Page posts, comments and other engagement information, messages sent to a connected Page, and Page insights or advertising information. Information is used to provide requested Page-management functions, such as viewing Page activity, preparing or publishing content when authorized, helping respond to comments or messages, and displaying available performance information. Information may be processed or stored only as needed to operate the app, maintain its security, and complete your requests. Access tokens and other credentials must be protected and must not be published or shared. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

We retain information only for as long as it is needed for the purposes described above or as required by law. You can request deletion by following our User Data Deletion instructions. You can also remove the app's access through your Facebook settings; revoking access may not by itself delete information already held by the app operator.

Your choices

You can stop using the app, disconnect its access through Facebook settings, and contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information that we control.
