User Data Deletion Instructions

Sponsor Pro Dz AI Manager

You may request deletion of personal information associated with your use of Sponsor Pro Dz AI Manager.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to sponsorprodz1@gmail.com with the subject Data Deletion Request.
  2. Tell us the Facebook Page name and, if available, the account or app connection involved. Do not email your password, access token, or other login credentials.
  3. We may ask for reasonable information to verify that you are authorized to make the request.

What happens next

After verifying the request, we will identify personal information under our control that is associated with the app, delete it where applicable, and reply to confirm completion or explain any information we must retain by law. We aim to respond within 30 days.

Disconnecting the app

You can also remove the app's access in your Facebook account settings under Business Integrations (where available). Disconnecting access stops future access through that authorization, but does not automatically delete information already stored by the app operator. Email us separately to request deletion.
